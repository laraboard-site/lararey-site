Privacy Policy

Interim privacy notice. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Who we are

This policy covers lararey.com, Lara Rey's conversations on Telegram (@LaraReyPrivateBot) and her public replies on Instagram, Facebook and Threads.

Lara Rey is a brand name. It is not a company: the service is run by an individual operator, not by a separately registered legal entity.

For anything about your data, including a request to know who is legally responsible for it, write to contact@lararey.com.

The website

lararey.com sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies, and it does not ask you for any information.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the technical data needed to deliver a web page, such as your IP address, and handles it under its own privacy statement.

Links to Instagram, Threads, TikTok and Telegram open those services. What they collect is covered by their own policies.

Talking with Lara on Telegram

Telegram gives us your Telegram account number, your name and username as you set them, and the messages and photos you send Lara or post in The Lounge. Your messages also pass through Telegram, which handles them under its own privacy policy.

Your answer to the first question (that you are 18 or older and understand that Lara is an AI character) is saved with the time and the version of the wording you saw.

For the private conversation you give an email address. We send a one-time code to it through Google's Gmail service, and we use it to confirm the address and to give the free first month only once per person.

We keep your messages, the photos you send her, and what Lara remembers from them, so that she can continue your conversations. There is no fixed deletion date: they stay until you delete them (see Your choices). Messages in The Lounge are also seen by its members.

The people who run Lara can read conversations, to look after the service, fix problems and improve how Lara writes.

How Lara's replies are made

Lara's replies are written by AI language models. To write a reply, the relevant part of your conversation (and any photo you sent) is sent to OpenAI or processed by open models that run on our own computers. OpenAI handles what it receives under its own terms and privacy policy.

Public comments on Instagram, Facebook and Threads

When you comment on Lara's public posts or reply to her there, we receive what those platforms show publicly: your username and account number on that platform and the text of your comment.

Lara reads these comments and decides whether to reply. To do that, the comment is processed by the same AI models as above. We keep the comments and her replies, and for each person a short note of what they talked about publicly with her, so she can follow a conversation. This is kept per platform, only from public comments, and is never joined with your Telegram conversation.

If you ask her to stop, she does not reply to you again. To have these records erased, write to contact@lararey.com with your username and the platform; /delete in Telegram does not cover them.

Payments

Purchases are made inside Telegram with Telegram Stars. We receive Telegram's payment confirmation (the plan, the amount in Stars, the time and Telegram's payment reference). We never receive card details. Telegram keeps its own record of the payment under its own terms.

If you ask for payment help with /paysupport, the message you send next is saved for us with your recent payment references. Anything that looks like a card number is removed first.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information and we do not use your conversations for advertising.

Your choices

Send /exit to withdraw your 18+ answer and close the chat. What Lara remembers stays until you delete it.

Send /delete, then DELETE, to erase what we hold about you: your messages and photos, what Lara remembers, your email address, your choices and your payment records. You are also taken out of The Lounge. What remains is a note that a deletion was made (counts only, no content) and two scrambled markers (made from your Telegram account and your email address, from which neither can be read back) so that the free month is not given twice. If you answered that you are under 18, that answer is kept for 7 days so the question is not simply asked again.

Deleting with us does not delete your own copy of the chat in Telegram, or what you posted in The Lounge as other members see it in Telegram; you can delete those in Telegram.

Our own backups are replaced on a rolling basis (the latest 14 are kept), so deleted information leaves them over time.

To ask what we hold about you, or for any other privacy request, write to contact@lararey.com.

Under 18

Lara's conversations are for people aged 18 or older. Anyone who answers that they are under 18 is not let in.

Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new date. Last updated: 16 November 2026.
